## Risu extensions

### [Risu Extensions](https://github.com/jindeveloper-ai/risu-extensions-portfolio)

**Extensions for Risu, an AI chat frontend.**
Listen to conversations as audiobooks, explore a response's inner thoughts, and adjust the reading environment.

- **[Audio Book Maid](https://github.com/jindeveloper-ai/risu-extensions-portfolio/tree/main/projects/audio-book-maid)**: Assign voices, review scripts, generate audio, and resume listening.
- **[Doki Doki](https://github.com/jindeveloper-ai/risu-extensions-portfolio/tree/main/projects/doki)**: Generate, save, and reopen inner thoughts for a selected response.
